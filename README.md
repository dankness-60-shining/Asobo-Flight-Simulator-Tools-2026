## 🎮 About the Game

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian flight simulation game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Xbox Game Studios. The PC version launched on August 18, 2020, and the free 40th Anniversary Edition update released on November 11, 2022. It combines realistic aircraft systems and flight physics with a worldwide streamed environment, dynamic weather, live air traffic, flight planning, helicopters, gliders, and historic aircraft.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020) 40th Anniversary Edition**. This trainer provides optional controls for reducing routine simulation constraints during free flight. It can help pilots manage fuel, protect the aircraft from damage, stabilize environmental conditions, control simulation speed, and recover from difficult situations without replacing the game's aircraft handling, navigation, or cockpit systems.

**Target Audience:** PC pilots, newcomers learning aircraft controls, screenshot and sightseeing enthusiasts, and experienced simmers who want more flexible practice flights without changing the core flight model.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Fuel** — Prevents usable aircraft fuel from being depleted during a flight, making long-distance routes and extended practice sessions easier.
- **Aircraft Damage Protection** — Reduces or prevents damage caused by hard landings, overstress, collisions, and other simulated aircraft hazards.
- **Simulation Speed Control** — Provides convenient controls for slowing down or accelerating the simulation during cruise segments, navigation practice, and approach preparation.

### 📌 Environment

- **Freeze Flight Time** — Locks the simulation clock at its current time so the lighting conditions remain consistent during training or sightseeing.
- **Stable Weather** — Maintains the current weather conditions instead of allowing changing wind, visibility, clouds, or precipitation to disrupt a practice flight.

### 📌 Accessibility

- **Flight Recovery** — Offers a quick recovery option for returning to a controlled flight state after an unstable attitude, missed approach, or difficult landing.

### 📌 Navigation

- **Position Lock** — Temporarily holds the aircraft's current position while the pilot reviews instruments, flight plans, scenery, or cockpit procedures.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Microsoft Flight Simulator uses aircraft controls rather than character movement. Select an aircraft and departure point from the World Map, then control pitch, roll, yaw, throttle, landing gear, flaps, brakes, and autopilot through the cockpit, controller, keyboard, or flight hardware. Position Lock is useful when studying instruments or preparing a procedure. Simulation Speed Control is best used during cruise and should be returned to normal before complex maneuvers or landing.

### Tools & Inventory

The simulator has no conventional character inventory or item-collection system. Aircraft equipment is represented by functional cockpit systems, fuel, payload, avionics, landing gear, flaps, and other configurable flight settings. The trainer's fuel option affects the aircraft's fuel resource and does not create or manage inventory items.

### World & Environment

Use the World Map to choose airports, departure and arrival procedures, aircraft, flight conditions, weather, and route information. The game represents locations across the planet and supports live or preset weather, time-of-day settings, air traffic, and multiplayer features. Freeze Flight Time and Stable Weather are intended for controlled practice in this dynamic environment.

### Combat & Weapons

Microsoft Flight Simulator is a civilian flight simulator and has no standard combat, weapons, enemy, or character battle system. Trainer options apply to aircraft operation, environmental conditions, and flight recovery rather than combat encounters.
